NYC Dance

NYC Dance is a dance studio teaching ballet and modern dances for all ages. From classical ballet regiments to jazz ballet and modern experimental dances, professional dancers share their knowledge and skills, while regularly performing at the studio. The studio is located in Mercer Street in Soho NYC.



Their Spring/Summer 2025 campaign aspired to be vibrant to not only reflect the seasons, but also the studio's atmosphere.



NYC Dance appreciates and cherishes ballet for being a classical art form, however, also intends to introduce it to a wider & perhaps more modern audience.
