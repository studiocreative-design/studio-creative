Ramen Shop

Ramen Shop is a small Japanese-own business serving authentic & specialty ramen dishes. Apart from the original (red) version, gluten-free (chartreuse) & vegan (aqua) options are also available. A strict colour-coding ensures clarity for all and safety for people with dietary restrictions.



Ramen Shop has two locations: one permanent in Richmond with eat-in or take-away ("city" or "go") options; and one temporary at the Arts Centre ("pop-up").
