Saturnalia Festival

Saturnalia Festival takes its name and draws inspiration from an ancient Roman festival of the same name. The new version of the festival is a 3-day cultural festival from the third Saturday of December each year, which includes events of ritual, music, dance, performance art, creative & personal development workshops and elaborate feasting & drinking.



The festival is timed to fall on or very close to the southern hemisphere summer solstice on December 21st, often celebrated as Midsummer in many European traditions.



This version of Saturnalia is a small to medium-sized camping festival held just outside Melbourne at the Hanging Rock regional setting. The event expects ticket sales of 3,000 capacity and is targeted towards 25-50 year-olds. It is an 18+ event only and minors are not permitted.





Feast in a another realm of being...

Saturnalia Festival draws participants into ancient solstice rituals, exploring the links between early & current myths, humans & nature, dark & light, birth & death, and different forms of human identity through a mix of creative and personal development workshops, performance art, music, feasts of food and drink, and transcendent states of being.
