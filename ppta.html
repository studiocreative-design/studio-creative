PPTA Pitch

The Plasma Protein Therapeutics Association (PPTA) is a dynamic trade association that represents a unique sector of the biologics and biotechnology industry. PPTA represents more than 1,100 human plasma collection centres in North America and Europe, as well as the manufacturers of lifesaving plasma protein therapies.


They have commissioned Yumeda, a design start-up, to create a new website, and for reaffirming the commitement of the two parties a new design pitch was necessary which needed to include new insights from an in-depth interview with the project coordinator from PPTA.


A key element of the updated requirements was that PPTA preferred the inclusion of a 3D element into their website.


Additionally, their primary goal was to stay informative and educational regarding plasma proteins and their importance.
