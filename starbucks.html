UX/UI Projects

Starbucks Kiosk

The Starbucks self-service kiosk designed for in-store use caters to both members and new customers while creating a fast-paced order service without the need for assistance or waiting in line.



The final focus of the project was on the member target group and their need for a swift and personalised ordering process.



The kiosk allows a smooth & secure sign-in process via the Starbucks mobile app that members would already have as it is needed for Starbucks' reward system. Once signed-in, members can have a faster and more tailored experience.
