Melbourne Music Festival

The Melbourne Music Festival (MMF) is not just about music, it is one of the main cultural events in Melbourne. The MMF app reflects the diversity of the programs and events ocurring in and around Melbourne while encouraging festival goers to get to know and immerse themselves in this vibrant city.



The MMF app's primary roles are: 1) easy & fast purchase of festival tickets & merchandise; 2) the direct registration of the festival wristbands; 3) easy overview of festival lineup & schedule with the option of creating own festival schedule.



The MMF app's secondary role is to provide an easy overview of other cultural events & their schedule with the option of creating own schedule that is synced with the festival's chosen events & concerts.
