Welcome to Studio Creative

Philosophy & Approach

Studio Creative is a design portfolio & a platform for sharing, connecting and collaborating.


Studio Creative displays creativity, curiosity, committement, care and calmness.



Studio Creative brings creativity into projects and everyday life by staying curious and committed; by caring; and by appreciating and prioritizing peace and calm.



Staying curious is paramount for continous learning; for fostering and keeping an open mind; for staying relevant and active; and for self-developement.


Showing care for all things around us, whether living or not, is pivotal in leading a respectful and mindful life.


Staying committed to our planet; to our environment; and to our bright future by fostering sustainable practices and design whenever and wherever possible.


Serenity, peacefulness and patience are crucial for creating appealing aesthetics; and for leading a tranquil and calm life.

Meet the Designer

I am Szilvi Cseke, the designer behind SC, nice to meet you here!


I am based in Europe, trained in Australia and completed projects worldwide.


Trained at Monash University as an Interaction Designer, I have completed several individual and group projects across a diverse range of areas such as ux/ui design, branding, 3D design, creative coding and co-design.


Internships at various European start-ups contributed an essential and incomparable experience within the industry. As a ux/ui design intern, I carried out web design, ux/ui audits and worked on several design pitches for clients. As a user research intern, I prepared and conducted client interviews, analysed the collected data and supported the design departmentwith insight and technical knowledge.


My main interests today are co-design, human-centred design, creative direction and ux/ui design.


Fun fact, I have not always been a designer! I started out as a marine biologist and worked as a researcher for years focusing on coral reefs and tropical marine ecology. This previous experience equipped me with many valuable transferable skills for my design work today. Nevertheless, I have learned to be flexible, resilient, and became an excellent communicator across different disciplines, languages and cultures.


In my free time, I like to do yoga, go for a walk (ocassionally a run) in nature and learn new languages. I love photography, visiting museums and art installations and cannot miss a cultural festival (or a Carnaval).


I like travelling, but I am rather a slow and mindful explorer than a tourist as I prefer to spend more time at a place in order to get to know the local people, language, culture and cuisine. Consequently, I have lived in Brazil, Spain, Italy, Australia, The UK and Germany.



Thank you for reading,

Szilvi
