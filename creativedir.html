Creative Direction

Voice In (VI) is a student initiative at Monash University; advised by the Excellence, Inclusion and Diversity (EID) Committee of the Chemical and Biological Engineering Department at the University.



VI believes the ability to articulate thoughts across to others is an essential skill that everyone should be equipped with.



VI intends to deliver a toolset for students and beyond in order for them to be able to confidently express themselves in various social and professional situations.





Upon joining Voice In, a complete branding was carried out along with setting up strategies for outreach & future projects.



along with the rebranding of the existing logo, a complete new design system was created that would be able to suit the brand and its expanding roles and products in the future. Beside the Brand Identity Guideline, an Introduction Guide was also created that contained a more concise and clear version of the brand's mission, vision & values together with the planned projects. This intro material was sent out to other initiatives and collaborators to facilitate an understanding around the new brand and to avoid any misunderstandings or misalignements.



In the first stage, there were four support videos that students could access. In the second stage, a more dynamic approach was adopted and workshops were planned and carried out. In order to be able to create practical and relatable content for these events, we needed to carry out thorough user research and increase our outreach.



Participatory research methods and follow-up interviews were selected as the appropriate approach.
For recruiting participants, a series of posters were placed on different locations on campus along with an extensive social media campaignon Facebook and Instagram.



Analysis of data helped inform us about the needs of our potential users and made it possible to create an updated and more extensive future strategy for the brand.





Collaborations with other student initiatives and clubs were initiated and re-initiated.
Beside creating a few strong connections, a workshop was created in collaboration with TEDxMonash on the subject of Networking & Soft Challenges in Professional Settings.
Following the success of this event, further workshops were facilitated and planned by Voice In.





After gaining more followers and more recognition, another social media campaign was launched to explain more of what Voice In is, what it plans to do, and how it wants to achieve it.

Branding Basics

Branding for Voice In started out with an old logo and a lot of brainstorming to find what would truly represent the brand and what it desires to stand for.



Their previous logo was a loudspeaker and we decided to keep that triangle shape in which we included the initials (VI) of the brand.



Three parallel lines were chosen to draw these initials; lines that represented different people, ideas and perspectives coming together and moving alongside each other.
Communicating. Evolving.



Two primary colours (yellow & blue) were chosen as secondary/accent brand colours based on colour psychology and the preference on the client. However, the primary brand colours (cream & black) remained clean, modern and restrained.



Montserrat and American Typewriter were chosen to represent the brand in order to keep it clean & modern with a bit of a human & playful touch.
